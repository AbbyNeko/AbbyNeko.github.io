Quest of Corona is an RPG game based on the hard times of 2020. Inspired by games like Pokemon, Legend of Zelda and DnD, the Quest of Corona is a plot-driven RPG game where you can play as multiple character classes. The game has its own database of users and it requires you to sign up for a free acount to play. The game itself has 3 levels and you only have 1 heal and 1 block per level. It is not a long game but my team may plan to expand this in the future.

Quest of Corona was most notably featured in the UCF Bootcamp Alumni Blog.
